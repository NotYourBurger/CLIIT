## Decisions

- The verb calls check.py directly from cli.py rather than routing through
  issues.py. `init` is the precedent: a verb that shares none of the filtering,
  blocking or ordering opinions issues.py exists to hold has no reason to be in
  it, and nothing may import issues.py anyway.
- Byte comparison, as the issue asks, not dict comparison. It flags whitespace
  a rewrite would normalise as well as prose a parser would eat - and the
  probe below says that costs nothing today.
- The field order `write_issue` writes is now `storage.FIELD_ORDER`, read by
  both. Copying the tuple into check.py would have made this check report a
  false finding on every file in the repo the day one of them changed - which
  is the exact drift the check exists to catch.
- Findings are grouped by file rather than by kind of check: two things wrong
  with one issue are two lines together.

## Discoveries

- All 31 files in the real .issues/ are already byte-stable through
  split_file -> join_file, so the strict comparison is the affordable one. A
  dict comparison would have been the fallback if they were not.
